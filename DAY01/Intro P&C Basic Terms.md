Types of Losses:
1. Direct
2. Indirect/Consequential

1. Direct Physical Loss: Immediate cause of damage. eg. Hail (storm) damage to home's roof
2. Indirect and Consequential Loss: Damage as a result of direct loss
    eg. Hail storm damage to home, as a result move to hotel
     i. Hail: Direct physical loss
     ii. Hotel expense: Indirect loss

Intervening cause: Interrupts chain of causation. Eg. you are in accident waiting for police to come, mean while tree fell on your car
Proximate cause: primary cause of loss. eg. Ligtning stuck and window is broken
