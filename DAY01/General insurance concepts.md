7. Frank sold his car to Jill but did not cancel his auto insurance policy. Jill had an accident one month later, and Frank reported the accident to his insurance company.
   Frank was not able to collect money for damages to the car, even though his policy was still in force because:
   a. Frank was not the individual who had the accident
   b. The insurance company did not follow proper claims procedure.
   c. Frank misrepresented that he still owned the vehicle by keeping the policy in force
   d. Frank no longer had insurable interest

8. Which of the following is NOT an example of insurable interest.?
   a. An individual's interest in property their parents told him they have left to him in a will.
   b. A tenant's interest in improvements they have made to a rental building
   c. A cleaner's interest in a customer's clothing that is still at the cleaner's facility
   d. An individual's interest in property being bought through a loan

Answers:
1. a (Indemnity is getting insured back to same financial position)
2. c (It protects applicant once he gets into accident one week later after sale)
3. b (Pure risk is insurable and not specualtive)
4. a (Identify act, act is Tornado which is the proximate (also PRIMARY) cause)
5. b
6. d (all the options are close, but primary is pay claims covered)
7. d (you have to be in a position to loose something financially)
8. a (why, cuz it has not been done yet)
