# Property & Casualty Insurance - Practice Quiz & Answer Key (Part 2)

### Question 1
**A stock insurance company is owned by its:**

- [ ] a. board of directors
- [ ] b. members
- [ ] c. policyholders
- [x] **d. stockholders**

> **Explanation:** A stock insurance company is owned by stockholders who purchase shares of stock as an investment.

---

### Question 2
**Melanie sells an insurance policy from Outlier Insurance, which is not authorized to transact insurance in her state. Her client later files a claim that Outlier wrongfully refuses to pay. Melanie may be required to:**

- [ ] a. force Outlier to pay the claim
- [ ] b. help Outlier become an authorized insurer
- [x] **c. pay the amount of the unpaid claim**
- [ ] d. sue Outlier

> **Explanation:** A producer who sells coverage from an unauthorized entity may be liable for unpaid claims, subject to regulatory penalties, and even imprisoned for committing a felony.

---

### Question 3
**Which kind of insurer involves mutual agreements to indemnify the insureds from loss?**

- [ ] a. Lloyd's association
- [ ] b. Mutual insurer
- [x] **c. Reciprocal insurer**
- [ ] d. Stock insurer

> **Explanation:** Sometimes called reciprocal exchanges, a reciprocal insurer is an unincorporated group of members that insure each other by exchanging contracts of indemnity that obligate them to collectively pay any member's covered loss.

---

### Question 4
**A mutual insurance company is owned by its:**

- [ ] a. board of directors
- [ ] b. members
- [x] **c. policyholders**
- [ ] d. stockholders

> **Explanation:** A mutual insurance company is owned by the people and organizations that own insurance policies which the insurance company has issued.

### Question 1
**In a standard insurance sales transaction involving an applicant, a producer, and an insurance company, which party is the offeree?**

- [ ] a. applicant
- [ ] b. beneficiary
- [x] **c. insurer**
- [ ] d. producer

> **Explanation:** In the typical insurance transaction, the applicant (offeror) makes the offer to the insurance company (offeree) through a signed application plus a premium deposit.

---

### Question 2
**If the courts find an ambiguity in an insurance policy, they will interpret it in the policyholder's favor because insurance policies are:**

- [ ] a. aleatory contracts
- [x] **b. contracts of adhesion**
- [ ] c. indemnity contracts
- [ ] d. personal contracts

> **Explanation:** Insurance contracts are contracts of adhesion, and if the courts find that a contract of adhesion is ambiguous, they will interpret it in favor of the party that did not write the contract.

---

### Question 3
**The fact that insurance policy language is written by an insurance company and is nonnegotiable makes insurance policies:**

- [ ] a. aleatory contracts
- [ ] b. conditional contracts
- [x] **c. contracts of adhesion**
- [ ] d. unilateral contracts

> **Explanation:** Most insurance contracts are contracts of adhesion because the policy is drafted by the insurer and little if any negotiation is normally available with respect to the policy's wording.

### Question 1
**Evelyn, who lives alone, never locks the door to her apartment when she goes to work. This is an example of a:**

- [ ] a. Legal hazard
- [ ] b. Moral hazard
- [x] **c. Morale hazard**
- [ ] d. Physical hazard

> **Explanation:** Not locking a car door or a house door is a morale hazard because Evelyn's carelessness or indifference increases the likelihood of a theft.

---

### Question 2
**Collette lives in a home on the ocean that is insured for $20 million. Although her insurance company would not be able to cover a $20 million loss with its own resources, the insurer is able to write a single policy with a $20 million limit for Collette because the insurance company has:**

- [ ] a. A large deductible in its policy
- [ ] b. Competition from the California Department of Insurance
- [x] **c. Reinsurance**
- [ ] d. Secured credit

> **Explanation:** By purchasing reinsurance, the insurer transfers some of its risk to a reinsurance company, and that makes it possible for the insurer to handle this large account.

---

### Question 3
**Which of the following is NOT a cost associated with operating an insurance system?**

- [ ] a. Cost of capital investment
- [ ] b. Cost of labor
- [ ] c. Increased losses due to attitudinal hazards
- [x] **d. Reduced crime rates**

> **Explanation:** The costs associated with operating an insurance system include land, capital investment, and labor, as well as increased losses due to attitudinal and moral hazards. Reduced crime rates are not a direct cost of the system.
