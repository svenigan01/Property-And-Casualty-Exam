# Property & Casualty Insurance - Practice Quiz & Answer Key

### Question 1
**The roof of Robbie's home was badly damaged by hail and had to be replaced. Fortunately, Robbie's insurance covered losses caused by hail. In this situation, hail is a(n):**

- [ ] a. exposure
- [ ] b. hazard
- [x] **c. peril**
- [ ] d. risk

> **Explanation:** A peril is a cause of loss. Hail, fire, windstorm, and theft are classic examples of perils.

---

### Question 2
**Theodore pays an annual premium to the insurance company that covers many of the risks associated with his business. What risk management technique is Theodore using?**

- [ ] a. risk avoidance
- [ ] b. risk control
- [ ] c. risk sharing
- [x] **d. risk transfer**

> **Explanation:** Using insurance to cover a risk is a classic example of transferring a risk. The insured party with the risk transfers it to the insurance company in exchange for a premium.

---

### Question 3
**Because the Bender Insurance Company insures a large number of similar convenience stores against fire:**

- [x] **a. It can predict its losses with much more accuracy than if it insured only a few stores.**
- [ ] b. It faces a heightened risk of a catastrophic loss.
- [ ] c. It is especially vulnerable to adverse selection.
- [ ] d. It is using the risk management technique of risk avoidance.

> **Explanation:** Bender benefits from the **Law of Large Numbers**. Insuring a large number of similar exposure units substantially improves an insurer's ability to predict future losses accurately.

---

### Question 4
**Which of the following is an example of risk transfer?**

- [ ] a. Derek decides not to buy a motorcycle. *(Risk Avoidance)*
- [ ] b. Leslie shovels her sidewalk immediately after every snowstorm. *(Risk Control / Reduction)*
- [ ] c. Lucia has a burglar alarm installed in her home. *(Risk Control / Reduction)*
- [x] **d. Sybil buys an auto insurance policy.**

> **Explanation:** By buying insurance, Sybil transfers certain financial risks to an insurance company in exchange for paying a premium.

### Question 2
**Which of the following conditions in an apartment building is a physical hazard?**

- [ ] a. fire
- [ ] b. landlord with a bad attitude
- [x] **c. slippery stairways**
- [ ] d. stained flooring

> **Explanation:** A hazard is a condition that increases the likely number of losses or the likely severity of a loss. Slippery stairways are a physical hazard because they increase the likelihood that someone could slip and be injured.

### Question 4
**Insuring a new restaurant against business failure is not possible because business success or failure is:**

- [ ] a. a pure risk
- [x] **b. a speculative risk**
- [ ] c. impossible to predict
- [ ] d. not measurable

> **Explanation:** A new business may succeed or fail, making it a speculative risk, which is not insurable. Only pure risks are insurable.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - General insurance principles - Insurance Providers

### Question 1
**Wendy reads in the newspaper that the insurance company that provides one of her policies is an admitted insurer in her state, and she asks her agent what this means. The agent tells her this means that the insurance company:**

- [ ] a. has pleaded guilty to violating the state's insurance laws
- [ ] b. is domiciled in another state
- [x] **c. is licensed to transact insurance business in her state**
- [ ] d. is subject to an impaired financial rating

> **Explanation:** An admitted insurer is a company that is licensed to do business in the state or country where the insured exposure is located.

---

### Question 2
**A stock insurance company is owned by its:**

- [ ] a. board of directors
- [ ] b. members
- [ ] c. policyholders
- [x] **d. stockholders**

> **Explanation:** A stock insurance company is owned by stockholders who purchase shares of stock as an investment.

---

### Question 3
**Melanie sells an insurance policy from Outlier Insurance, which is not authorized to transact insurance in her state. Her client later files a claim that Outlier wrongfully refuses to pay. Melanie may be required to:**

- [ ] a. force Outlier to pay the claim
- [ ] b. help Outlier become an authorized insurer
- [x] **c. pay the amount of the unpaid claim**
- [ ] d. sue Outlier

> **Explanation:** A producer who sells coverage from an unauthorized entity may be liable for unpaid claims, subject to regulatory penalties, and even imprisoned for committing a felony.

---

### Question 4
**VWX Insurance Company is considered an alien insurer, which means it is domiciled:**

- [ ] a. in a different state
- [ ] b. in the commissioner's state
- [ ] c. on the Internet
- [x] **d. outside the United States**

> **Explanation:** A company that is incorporated in a country outside the United States and is doing business in the United States is classified as an alien insurer.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - General insurance principles - Insurance Producers

### Question 1
**Making sure a recommended insurance product matches a customer's needs and circumstances means determining the product's:**

- [ ] a. credibility
- [ ] b. marketability
- [ ] c. portability
- [x] **d. suitability**

> **Explanation:** The suitability standard requires an insurance agent to be sure a product sold to a consumer appears to suit the needs of that consumer.

---

### Question 2
**An insurance producer is required to act in whose best interest?**

- [x] **a. applicant or insured**
- [ ] b. insurance commissioner
- [ ] c. National Association of Insurance Commissioners
- [ ] d. producer's office staff

> **Explanation:** In addition to the duties an agent owes to the insurer, the agent is also required to act in the best interests of the applicant or insured.

---

### Question 3
**As an insurance agent, Billy owes certain duties to the people he insures and those who apply for insurance. Which of the following is not one of those duties?**

- [ ] a. Billy must disclose pertinent information concerning a policy he proposes to provide.
- [ ] b. Billy must place his clients' insurance with a solvent insurer.
- [x] **c. Billy must propose the most expensive coverage the applicant can afford.**
- [ ] d. Billy must provide the policies that were agreed on.

> **Explanation:** An agent has a duty to provide the agreed-on policies, to place them with a solvent insurer, and to disclose all pertinent information. However, there is no duty to sell or even to recommend the most expensive coverage unless it is also the coverage most suitable for that particular client.

---

### Question 4
**All the following are regarded as illegal practices by insurance producers EXCEPT:**

- [x] **a. explaining the terms or conditions of a recommended insurance policy**
- [ ] b. false advertising
- [ ] c. misrepresentation
- [ ] d. unfair discrimination

> **Explanation:** Producers are expected to provide consumers with accurate information and avoid all forms of misrepresentation, deceptive or false advertising, and unfair discrimination.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Practice Quiz & Answer Key (Part 31)

### Question 1
**In a standard insurance sales transaction involving an applicant, a producer, and an insurance company, which party is the offeree?**

- [ ] a. applicant
- [ ] b. beneficiary
- [x] **c. insurer**
- [ ] d. producer

> **Explanation:** In the typical insurance transaction, the applicant (offeror) makes the offer to the insurance company (offeree) through a signed application plus a premium deposit.

---

### Question 2
**When applying for a businessowners insurance policy, Brandy did not mention the hazardous and illegal activity that goes on in the backroom of her shop because she realized no insurer that knew about it would insure her business. What is Brandy's failure to disclose this activity considered?**

- [ ] a. a waiver
- [x] **b. concealment**
- [ ] c. estoppel
- [ ] d. misrepresentation

> **Explanation:** The deliberate withholding of material facts is concealment.

---

### Question 3
**Which of the following correctly explains why insurance policies are contracts of adhesion?**

- [ ] a. Federal insurance regulators determine the wording of the policy.
- [x] **b. The insurance company determines the wording of the policy.**
- [ ] c. The loss exposure is usually greater than the premium.
- [ ] d. The premium is usually greater than the loss exposure.

> **Explanation:** With an insurance contract, the insurer invariably determines the policy's wording, and the insurance buyer who wants that policy has to "adhere," or stick, to the policy's written provisions.

---

### Question 4
**If the courts find an ambiguity in an insurance policy, they will interpret it in the policyholder's favor because insurance policies are:**

- [ ] a. aleatory contracts
- [x] **b. contracts of adhesion**
- [ ] c. indemnity contracts
- [ ] d. personal contracts

> **Explanation:** Insurance contracts are contracts of adhesion, and if the courts find that a contract of adhesion is ambiguous, they will interpret it in favor of the party that did not write the contract.
