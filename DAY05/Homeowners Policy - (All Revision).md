# CA Property and Casualty Agent Exam - Homeowners Policy - Homeowners Policies, Part 1

---

### Question 1
**Which ISO homeowners insurance form provides open perils coverage on the dwelling and named perils coverage on personal property?**

- [ ] **a.** HO 2
- [x] **b. HO 3**
- [ ] **c.** HO 4
- [ ] **d.** HO 5

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **b**  
> The HO 3 (Special Form) insures the dwelling and other structures on an open perils basis, and personal property on a named perils basis.

---

### Question 2
**Rocco bought his home before he married Crystal. The declarations page of his homeowners insurance policy refers to him as the named insured. The "you" throughout the policy will refer to:**

- [ ] **a.** Any insured person
- [ ] **b.** Rocco and all members of his immediate family
- [x] **c. Rocco and his wife, Crystal**
- [ ] **d.** The insurance company

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **c**  
> As defined in the homeowners insurance policy, "you" and "your" mean the named insured identified in the policy declarations and his or her spouse, provided they reside in the same household.

---

### Question 3
**As defined in a homeowners insurance policy, the broad term "motor vehicle" includes the following EXCEPT:**

- [ ] **a.** A motorcycle
- [ ] **b.** A motorized wheelchair
- [ ] **c.** A self-propelled walk-behind lawn mower
- [x] **d. An outboard motorboat**

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **d**  
> "Motor vehicle" is defined as a self-propelled land or amphibious vehicle or any trailer connected with it. This definition includes motorcycles, self-propelled lawn mowers, and motorized wheelchairs. An outboard motorboat is classified as a watercraft, not a motor vehicle.

---

### Question 4
**Owen's house is damaged when Latrice's car crashes into it. After Owen's insurer pays to repair the house, the insurer tries to recover its costs from Latrice. What provision in Owen's policy gives the insurer this right?**

- [ ] **a.** Assignment
- [ ] **b.** Loss settlement
- [ ] **c.** Recovered property
- [x] **d. Subrogation**

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **d**  
> The subrogation provision stipulates the insurer's rights to recover payments made to an insured from a negligent third party responsible for causing the loss.

---

### Question 1
**A chandelier falls into the crowd attending the bridal shower at Astrid and Tony's house. The medical payments to others coverage of Astrid and Tony's homeowners policy will pay for whose medical bills?**

- [ ] **a.** Astrid and Tony's pet dog, Silas
- [ ] **b.** Astrid's
- [x] **c. Bridget's, the honored guest at the party**
- [ ] **d.** Elvin's, the electrician who installed the chandelier

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **c**  
> Coverage applies to persons injured on the insured location who are there with the insured's permission. It does not cover the named insured, regular residents of the household, or pets. The electrician is also not covered by the homeowner's policy.

---

### Question 2
**Suzanne runs a business in her large house and employs several people on the premises. Who is a residence employee as defined in Suzanne's homeowners policy?**

- [ ] **a.** Bill, the secretary who handles Suzanne's business correspondence
- [ ] **b.** Gwendolyn, Suzanne's insurance agent
- [x] **c. Rachel, Suzanne's maid**
- [ ] **d.** Thanh, Suzanne's personal trainer

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **c**  
> Only Rachel is a residence employee because her duties are related to the maintenance or use of the residence premises. Employees handling business correspondence or personal services not related to household maintenance do not qualify.

---

### Question 3
**Lorna is a nanny who lives in Ronald's house and cares for his children. On her day off, she sprains her ankle while shopping for new shoes. With respect to her resulting medical expenses, the medical payments to others coverage of Ronald's policy will:**

- [ ] **a.** Provide coverage because she is a residence employee
- [ ] **b.** Provide coverage because she is a resident of the household
- [ ] **c.** Provide no coverage because she is eligible for workers compensation benefits for any job-related injury
- [x] **d. Provide no coverage because she was away from the insured location and injured outside the scope of her employment**

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **d**  
> The residence employee exclusion precludes medical payments coverage for a residence employee who is injured while away from the insured location and acting outside the scope of their employment.

---

### Question 4
**Marcus sells homemade jams and jellies from his home in a town that is popular with tourists. Marcus wants the broad protection of a home business insurance coverage (HOBIZ) endorsement. Which factor makes him ineligible for coverage under this endorsement?**

- [ ] **a.** He has a part-time employee.
- [ ] **b.** He is the sole owner of this business.
- [x] **c. His business involves the sale of food products.**
- [ ] **d.** His home is a residence.

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **c**  
> Businesses that involve the manufacture or sale of food products are specifically ineligible for the Home Business Insurance Coverage (HOBIZ) endorsement.
