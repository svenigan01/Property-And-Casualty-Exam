

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Property Insurance - Home Owner's Policy

### Question 1
**Eva bought a single-family home in an adult community that provides amenities that are supported by homeowners' association dues. The association has inadequate property insurance and charges Eva $750 for her share of payment for uninsured windstorm damage to the clubhouse. Which statement is correct about her HO 3 policy coverage for this expense?**

- [ ] a. Additional living expense coverage applies.
- [x] **b. Loss assessment additional coverage applies.**
- [ ] c. No coverage applies because Eva's house was not damaged.
- [ ] d. Ordinance or law additional coverage applies.

> **Explanation:** The loss assessment additional coverage applies when the association's insurance coverage is inadequate to cover a property loss, and the association assesses its members to pay for the damage.

---

### Question 2
**Edwina has a standard HO 3 policy with no added endorsements. Her diamond earrings are stolen and she notifies her insurance agent. However, her insurance covers only a portion of the loss because the policy contains a(n):**

- [ ] a. scheduled jewelry exclusion
- [ ] b. sublimit for any loss to jewelry
- [x] **c. sublimit for theft of jewelry**
- [ ] d. unscheduled jewelry exclusion

> **Explanation:** The HO 3 covers jewelry, but theft losses to jewelry are limited by a dollar sublimit.

---

### Question 3
**Why should a photographer schedule valuable camera equipment under a scheduled personal property endorsement to an HO 3 policy?**

- [x] **a. The endorsement covers some causes of loss that are not covered under a homeowners insurance policy.**
- [ ] b. The endorsement's actual cash value coverage on scheduled items is preferable to the homeowners insurance policy's replacement cost coverage.
- [ ] c. The homeowners insurance policy excludes theft coverage on cameras.
- [ ] d. The homeowners insurance policy has a sublimit on theft of cameras.

> **Explanation:** The endorsement provides coverage on an open perils basis, as compared to the named perils covered under an unendorsed HO 3 policy.

---

### Question 4
**Leopoldo's two-car garage is connected to his house by an enclosed breezeway. Which coverage of his homeowners insurance policy applies to his garage?**

- [x] **a. Coverage A**
- [ ] b. Coverage B
- [ ] c. Coverage C
- [ ] d. Coverage D

> **Explanation:** Besides covering the named insured's house, Coverage A also covers structures attached to the dwelling, and Leopoldo has an attached garage. If there were an open space instead of a breezeway between the house and the garage, the garage would be considered an "other structure" covered under Coverage B.

**Water runoff from Sam's air conditioner eventually stained the expensive tile blocks in his neighbor's patio. Barring an exclusion, Sam's homeowners policy will cover the property damage liability claim if it involves an occurrence. Is this an occurrence?**

- [ ] c. lets the insurer investigate the information in the application

**Ida's boutique at a shopping mall escapes damage when a portion of the atrium roof caves in. However, her business came to a sudden halt when the debris blocks access to her store. Does Ida's businessowners policy cover the resulting loss of income?**

> **Explanation:** Coverage applies to cessation of the insured's business activities due to uninhabitability of described premises. This includes any area used to gain access to the insured's premises.

### Question 1
**Which person would NOT be insured by a company's BOP liability coverage?**

- [ ] a. Melanie, wife of the named insured
- [ ] b. Michael, business partner of the named insured
- [x] **c. Pat, domestic servant for the named insured**
- [ ] d. Toni, board president for the insured business

> **Explanation:** If the named insured is an individual, the named insured and the insured's spouse are covered, but only with respect to the conduct of the business. If the insured is a partnership or joint venture, its members and partners and their spouses are covered, but only with respect to the conduct of the business.

### Question 2

- [ ] a. arise from the insured's legal liability
- [ ] b. arise within the policy period.

> **Explanation:** Business liability coverage covers sums the insured is legally obligated to pay for bodily injury, property damage, and personal and advertising injury. It covers losses arising within the policy period and within the coverage territory. It also covers continuous injury that occurs during more than one policy period.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Inland and Ocean Marine Insurance - Inland Marine Insurance

### Question 1
**Paving Contractor has a contractors' equipment floater that covers its paver and other scheduled equipment. If the paver is totally destroyed by a covered peril during the policy term, what will the insurer do?**

- [ ] b. pay the claim and cancel Paving Contractor's policy
- [x] **c. pay the claim and refund the unearned premium on the paver**
- [ ] d. pay the claim but make no refund of premium

> **Explanation:** The reinstatement of limit after loss provision in the common inland marine conditions form provides that, when there is a total loss to a scheduled item of property, the insurer will pay the claim and then refund the unearned premium on that item.

---

### Question 2
**Five years ago, Joe bought a piece of business equipment that he insured under a commercial inland marine floater. When a water leak damaged the equipment, its actual cash value was $1,000, but it would cost $5,000 to replace it. A qualified repairman bid $2,000 to repair the water damage. - [x] **a. $1,000**
- [ ] b. $2,000
- [ ] c. $5,000
- [ ] d. nothing

**When reading his livestock coverage form, Tad discovers that it excludes coverage for some perils he thought would be covered and covers some perils he thought would be excluded. For instance, he finds that the policy covers loss to covered livestock caused by:**

- [ ] a. earth movement
- [x] **b. flood**
- [ ] c. intentional loss
- [ ] d. neglect

> **Explanation:** The livestock coverage form covers named perils including fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, explosion, riot, aircraft, vehicles, smoke, falling objects, weight of ice/snow/sleet, collapse, theft, flood, and earth movement (earthquake). Standard exclusions like flood and earth movement are explicitly included as covered perils under the livestock coverage form, while usual exclusions like neglect and intentional loss remain excluded.

### Question 2
**When describing the policy's coverage, the word "special" on the declarations page means the policy is a(n):**

- [ ] a. basic causes of loss form
- [ ] b. broad causes of loss form
- [ ] c. modified causes of loss form
- [x] **d. open perils form**

> **Explanation:** In property and farm insurance terminology, a "special" causes of loss form provides open perils coverage (insuring against all direct physical loss unless specifically excluded), as opposed to basic or broad forms which are named perils forms.

---

### Question 3
**The Farm Property-Barns, Outbuildings and Other Farm Structures Coverage Form (FP 00 14) can cover all the following types of structures, EXCEPT:**

- [ ] a. barns
- [x] **b. field or pasture fences**
- [ ] c. outdoor antennas and antenna towers
- [ ] d. silos

> **Explanation:** Field or pasture fences are specifically excluded under Form FP 00 14 (they generally require specific endorsement or separate coverage, such as a specialized schedule or endorsement, because standard farm structure coverage forms exclude field/pasture fencing).

---

### Question 4
**Under the broad causes of loss form of a farm property policy, which of the following losses to livestock would NOT be covered?**

- [ ] a. A bull strays onto the highway where it is struck and killed by a Mack truck.

- [ ] c. Some livestock are electrocuted when a live power line falls into their pasture.
- [ ] d. Wolves attack and kill several of the farm's sheep.

> **Explanation:** Disease, illness, and natural causes of death (such as bovine flu) are excluded under property and farm causes of loss forms. > **Explanation:** Equipment breakdown insurance is intended to pay for unexpected breakdowns of equipment. It is not intended to pay routine expenses that are necessary to keep the equipment running properly.

### Question 3
**An equipment breakdown policy does NOT cover which of the following?**

- [ ] a. damage due to interruption of utilities
- [ ] b. damage due to spoilage
- [x] **c. damage from smoke**
- [ ] d. damage to data and media

> **Explanation:** An equipment breakdown policy excludes coverage for damage of equipment due to smoke. Damage to media and data is covered, though it is subject to sublimits.

### Question 4
**Which of the following is a coverage option that is available in a standard equipment breakdown policy?**

- [ ] a. corrosion
- [ ] b. internal dry rot
- [ ] c. smoke damage
- [x] **d. spoilage damage**

> **Explanation:** As long as the insured is legally responsible for the damaged property, spoilage damage is a covered peril. Dry rot, corrosion, and smoke damage are not coverage options.

### Question 4
**Agustin's building has an insurable value of $100,000, but he has insured it for only $80,000 in a property policy with a 90 percent coinsurance clause. If Agustin's building has a $10,000 loss caused by a covered peril, the insurer will pay him (ignore any deductible that might apply):**

- [ ] a. $10,000
