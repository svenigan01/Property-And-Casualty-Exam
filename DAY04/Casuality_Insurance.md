
# Property & Casualty Insurance - Casualty Insurance - Auto Insurance -  Regulation of Auto Insurance

### Question 1
**When the front fenders of Bobbie's coupe were damaged in an auto accident, his insurer arranged to replace them with non-original equipment manufacturer (non-OEM) parts. Why does the insurer use non-OEM parts?**

- [ ] a. to eliminate the deductible
- [ ] b. to ensure that the replacement parts are the best available
- [ ] c. to guarantee that the replacement parts are of like kind and quality
- [x] **d. to help minimize the cost of repairs and the cost of insurance**

> **Explanation:** Insurers' use of aftermarket parts from non-original equipment manufacturers helps minimize the cost of repairs and the cost of insurance.

---

### Question 2
**In an attempt to reduce her expenses, Mattie sells her car. What must she do to cancel her auto insurance policy?**

- [ ] a. forfeit the unearned premium
- [ ] b. nothing, because coverage automatically ended when she sold the car
- [x] **c. notify the insurer in writing of her intent to cancel the policy**
- [ ] d. pay a cancellation fee

> **Explanation:** The named insured can cancel by giving the insurer advance written notice of the intent to cancel the policy.

---

### Question 3
**Underinsured motorists (UIM) coverage protects an insured in an accident caused by a motorist who:**

- [x] **a. has coverage limits that are lower than the insured's UM/UIM limits**
- [ ] b. has no auto liability insurance
- [ ] c. is driving a rental car
- [ ] d. is not legally responsible for causing the accident

> **Explanation:** UIM coverage provides protection for the insured's bodily injury when an accident is caused by a motorist whose liability insurance limits are lower than the insured's UM/UIM limits.

---

### Question 4
**The driver's door on Misty's roadster was damaged in an auto accident. Her insurer wants to have the damage repaired with non-original equipment manufacturer parts. The state law is most likely to require the insurer to do all of the following EXCEPT:**

- [ ] a. disclose the use of non-OEM parts to Misty
- [ ] b. ensure that the parts identify their manufacturer
- [ ] c. get Misty's consent to use non-OEM parts
- [x] **d. waive the deductible**

> **Explanation:** Many states require that non-OEM parts carry permanent identification and that the insurer disclose and get the consumer's consent to the use of non-OEM parts.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Casualty Insurance - The Personal Auto Policy (PAP)

### Question 1
**Eliza's car is insured with a personal auto policy that includes liability, collision, and other-than-collision coverages. She buys a pickup truck. What coverage, if any, will the PAP provide for the truck?**

- [ ] a. automatic collision coverage if she reports the newly acquired vehicle to the insurer within 30 days
- [ ] b. automatic liability coverage for the remainder of the policy period
- [x] **c. automatic liability coverage if she reports the newly acquired vehicle to the insurer within 14 days.**
- [ ] d. none because the newly acquired vehicle is a truck

> **Explanation:** Because the truck is an additional vehicle, it has automatic liability coverage immediately. Eliza must notify the insurer within 14 days to add the truck to her policy.

---

### Question 2
**Which is least likely to be considered a "covered auto" in a personal auto policy (PAP)?**

- [x] **a. commercial tractor-trailer combination**
- [ ] b. newly acquired passenger auto
- [ ] c. owned private trailer
- [ ] d. temporary substitute passenger auto

> **Explanation:** The PAP provides only limited coverage for trucks with a GVW of 10,000 pounds or more. These types of vehicles should be insured on a commercial automobile policy if they are principally used to carry or deliver goods as part of a commercial operation.

---

### Question 3
**Which is NOT one of the three major parts of a personal auto policy (PAP)?**

- [x] **a. applicable endorsements**
- [ ] b. declarations page
- [ ] c. definitions
- [ ] d. personal auto coverage form

> **Explanation:** Although the PAP definitions are important to the policy, they are not one of its three major parts.

---

### Question 4
**Riley's coupe is insured with a personal auto policy that includes liability, uninsured motorists, and other-than-collision coverages. He buys a new sedan and wants collision coverage on it. Which statement is true about this coverage?**

- [x] **a. He must notify the insurer about the newly acquired sedan within four days.**
- [ ] b. The PAP automatically covers the sedan if he reports it to the insurer after owning it for two or three weeks.
- [ ] c. The PAP does not cover the automatic coverage for the Honda.
- [ ] d. The sedan automatically has collision coverage for the rest of the policy period.

> **Explanation:** Because Riley's coupe does not have collision coverage, he must report the newly acquired sedan to his insurer within four days of its acquisition to get collision coverage for it.
