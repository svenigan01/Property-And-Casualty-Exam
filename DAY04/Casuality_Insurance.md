
# Property & Casualty Insurance - Casualty Insurance - Auto Insurance -  Regulation of Auto Insurance

### Question 1
**When the front fenders of Bobbie's coupe were damaged in an auto accident, his insurer arranged to replace them with non-original equipment manufacturer (non-OEM) parts. Why does the insurer use non-OEM parts?**

- [ ] a. to eliminate the deductible
- [ ] b. to ensure that the replacement parts are the best available
- [ ] c. to guarantee that the replacement parts are of like kind and quality
- [x] **d. to help minimize the cost of repairs and the cost of insurance**

> **Explanation:** Insurers' use of aftermarket parts from non-original equipment manufacturers helps minimize the cost of repairs and the cost of insurance.

### Question 2
**In an attempt to reduce her expenses, Mattie sells her car. What must she do to cancel her auto insurance policy?**

- [ ] a. forfeit the unearned premium
- [ ] b. nothing, because coverage automatically ended when she sold the car
- [x] **c. notify the insurer in writing of her intent to cancel the policy**
- [ ] d. pay a cancellation fee

> **Explanation:** The named insured can cancel by giving the insurer advance written notice of the intent to cancel the policy.

---

### Question 3
**Underinsured motorists (UIM) coverage protects an insured in an accident caused by a motorist who:**

- [x] **a. has coverage limits that are lower than the insured's UM/UIM limits**
- [ ] b. has no auto liability insurance
- [ ] c. is driving a rental car
- [ ] d. is not legally responsible for causing the accident

> **Explanation:** UIM coverage provides protection for the insured's bodily injury when an accident is caused by a motorist whose liability insurance limits are lower than the insured's UM/UIM limits.

---

### Question 4
**The driver's door on Misty's roadster was damaged in an auto accident. Her insurer wants to have the damage repaired with non-original equipment manufacturer parts. The state law is most likely to require the insurer to do all of the following EXCEPT:**

- [ ] a. disclose the use of non-OEM parts to Misty
- [ ] b. ensure that the parts identify their manufacturer
- [ ] c. get Misty's consent to use non-OEM parts
- [x] **d. waive the deductible**

> **Explanation:** Many states require that non-OEM parts carry permanent identification and that the insurer disclose and get the consumer's consent to the use of non-OEM parts.
