
# Property & Casualty Insurance - Casualty Insurance - Auto Insurance -  Regulation of Auto Insurance

### Question 1
**When the front fenders of Bobbie's coupe were damaged in an auto accident, his insurer arranged to replace them with non-original equipment manufacturer (non-OEM) parts. Why does the insurer use non-OEM parts?**

- [ ] a. to eliminate the deductible
- [ ] b. to ensure that the replacement parts are the best available
- [ ] c. to guarantee that the replacement parts are of like kind and quality
- [x] **d. to help minimize the cost of repairs and the cost of insurance**

> **Explanation:** Insurers' use of aftermarket parts from non-original equipment manufacturers helps minimize the cost of repairs and the cost of insurance.

---

### Question 2
**In an attempt to reduce her expenses, Mattie sells her car. What must she do to cancel her auto insurance policy?**

- [ ] a. forfeit the unearned premium
- [ ] b. nothing, because coverage automatically ended when she sold the car
- [x] **c. notify the insurer in writing of her intent to cancel the policy**
- [ ] d. pay a cancellation fee

> **Explanation:** The named insured can cancel by giving the insurer advance written notice of the intent to cancel the policy.

---

### Question 3
**Underinsured motorists (UIM) coverage protects an insured in an accident caused by a motorist who:**

- [x] **a. has coverage limits that are lower than the insured's UM/UIM limits**
- [ ] b. has no auto liability insurance
- [ ] c. is driving a rental car
- [ ] d. is not legally responsible for causing the accident

> **Explanation:** UIM coverage provides protection for the insured's bodily injury when an accident is caused by a motorist whose liability insurance limits are lower than the insured's UM/UIM limits.

---

### Question 4
**The driver's door on Misty's roadster was damaged in an auto accident. Her insurer wants to have the damage repaired with non-original equipment manufacturer parts. The state law is most likely to require the insurer to do all of the following EXCEPT:**

- [ ] a. disclose the use of non-OEM parts to Misty
- [ ] b. ensure that the parts identify their manufacturer
- [ ] c. get Misty's consent to use non-OEM parts
- [x] **d. waive the deductible**

> **Explanation:** Many states require that non-OEM parts carry permanent identification and that the insurer disclose and get the consumer's consent to the use of non-OEM parts.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Casualty Insurance - The Personal Auto Policy (PAP)

### Question 1
**Eliza's car is insured with a personal auto policy that includes liability, collision, and other-than-collision coverages. She buys a pickup truck. What coverage, if any, will the PAP provide for the truck?**

- [ ] a. automatic collision coverage if she reports the newly acquired vehicle to the insurer within 30 days
- [ ] b. automatic liability coverage for the remainder of the policy period
- [x] **c. automatic liability coverage if she reports the newly acquired vehicle to the insurer within 14 days.**
- [ ] d. none because the newly acquired vehicle is a truck

> **Explanation:** Because the truck is an additional vehicle, it has automatic liability coverage immediately. Eliza must notify the insurer within 14 days to add the truck to her policy.

---

### Question 2
**Which is least likely to be considered a "covered auto" in a personal auto policy (PAP)?**

- [x] **a. commercial tractor-trailer combination**
- [ ] b. newly acquired passenger auto
- [ ] c. owned private trailer
- [ ] d. temporary substitute passenger auto

> **Explanation:** The PAP provides only limited coverage for trucks with a GVW of 10,000 pounds or more. These types of vehicles should be insured on a commercial automobile policy if they are principally used to carry or deliver goods as part of a commercial operation.

---

### Question 3
**Which is NOT one of the three major parts of a personal auto policy (PAP)?**

- [x] **a. applicable endorsements**
- [ ] b. declarations page
- [ ] c. definitions
- [ ] d. personal auto coverage form

> **Explanation:** Although the PAP definitions are important to the policy, they are not one of its three major parts.

---

### Question 4
**Riley's coupe is insured with a personal auto policy that includes liability, uninsured motorists, and other-than-collision coverages. He buys a new sedan and wants collision coverage on it. Which statement is true about this coverage?**

- [x] **a. He must notify the insurer about the newly acquired sedan within four days.**
- [ ] b. The PAP automatically covers the sedan if he reports it to the insurer after owning it for two or three weeks.
- [ ] c. The PAP does not cover the automatic coverage for the Honda.
- [ ] d. The sedan automatically has collision coverage for the rest of the policy period.

> **Explanation:** Because Riley's coupe does not have collision coverage, he must report the newly acquired sedan to his insurer within four days of its acquisition to get collision coverage for it.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Casualty Insurance - Liability Coverages of the Personal Auto Policy

### Question 1
**Phoebe rents her home. She loses control of her car on the icy driveway and crashes into the garage door. It will cost $1,000 to repair or replace the door. The property damage liability coverage of Phoebe's personal auto policy will:**

- [ ] a. not pay for the damage because she does not own the home
- [ ] b. not pay for the damage because the car was in Phoebe's care, custody, and control
- [x] **c. pay for the damage**
- [ ] d. pay for the damage to Phoebe's car but not for the damage to the garage

> **Explanation:** Although the PAP does not cover liability for damage to others' property that the insured possesses, it does cover such property when the insured rents a residence or private garage.

---

### Question 2
**The purpose of underinsured motorists (UIM) coverage is to:**

- [ ] a. pay the deductible for underinsured drivers whose autos are damaged
- [ ] b. protect insureds from drivers who have no auto liability insurance
- [x] **c. protect insureds from drivers with inadequate limits of auto liability insurance**
- [ ] d. protect underinsured drivers from claims brought by people they might injure

> **Explanation:** Underinsured motorists coverage protects insureds from drivers who do not have limits of auto liability insurance that are high enough to cover the insured's bodily injury claim.

---

### Question 3
**Which part of the personal auto policy (PAP) contains an insuring agreement that covers the named insured for the cost of legal representation in a lawsuit arising from an auto accident?**

- [x] **a. Part A-Liability Coverage**
- [ ] b. Part B-Medical Payments Coverage
- [ ] c. Part C-Uninsured/Underinsured Motorists Coverage
- [ ] d. Parts A, B, and C

> **Explanation:** The Part A insuring agreement covers the cost of defending the insured from a lawsuit or claim arising from an auto accident.

---

### Question 4
**Brian's car collides with Lola's car in the high school parking lot. She claims he hit her on purpose, but Brian insists the collision was accidental. What is true about his coverage under his PAP's liability insuring agreement?**

- [ ] a. Brian's insurer will defend him and deny payment if it is determined that he accidentally collided with Lola.
- [ ] b. The insurer has no obligation to protect Brian against allegations of intentional damage or to pay any related damages.
- [ ] c. The insurer is not obligated to defend Brian against allegations of intentional damage, but it will pay for the damage if he proves the collision was accidental.
- [x] **d. The insurer will defend Brian and pay damages if coverage applies.**

> **Explanation:** The duty to defend is broader than the duty to indemnify and extends to cases in which it is unclear whether coverage applies.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Casualty Insurance - Property Coverages of the Personal Auto Policy

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Practice Quiz & Answer Key (Part 37)

### Question 1
**Kendrick's car is insured with a personal auto policy (PAP). How can he have the policy also cover his new motorcycle?**

- [x] **a. add the miscellaneous type vehicle endorsement to the PAP**
- [ ] b. list the motorcycle in the PAP's declarations
- [ ] c. replace the PAP with a fleet policy to cover more than one vehicle
- [ ] d. the PAP provides automatic coverage on a newly acquired vehicle

> **Explanation:** The miscellaneous type vehicle endorsement broadens the definition of covered autos to include miscellaneous type vehicles, such as motorcycles.

---

### Question 2
**Tia and her sister Kara pool their summer earnings to buy a car and pay for a personal auto policy (PAP). How can the policy cover both sisters?**

- [x] **a. They must add the joint ownership coverage endorsement.**
- [ ] b. They must be named in the declarations.
- [ ] c. They must live in the same state.
- [ ] d. They must register the car's title in both names.

> **Explanation:** An unendorsed PAP does not recognize joint ownership except for a married couple. However, the joint ownership coverage endorsement provides coverage for a vehicle that is owned by two or more resident relatives.

---

### Question 3
**Joe's personal auto policy (PAP) has a $100 deductible for collision coverage on his sedan but no physical damage coverage on his old truck. When the truck is being repaired, Joe borrows Robin's new sports car and crashes it. Robin's collision coverage has a $1,000 deductible. Who will pay for the sports car's repairs?**

- [ ] a. Joe has no applicable collision coverage and must pay for all repairs.
- [ ] b. Joe's policy will pay all but $100, and Joe will pay $100.
- [ ] c. Robin's policy will pay all but $1,000 and Joe will pay $100.
- [x] **d. Robin's policy will pay all but $1,000, Joe's policy will pay $900, and Joe will pay $100.**

> **Explanation:** Because the sports car was a temporary substitute for an owned auto, Joe's policy considers it a nonowned auto, and Joe gets the broadest physical damage coverage available on any of his autos. However, because insurance follows the car, Robin's policy provides primary coverage.

---

### Question 4
**Maryann's car is being towed from its parking space on the street in front of her home. The other-than-collision coverage of her personal auto policy will cover her loss if the:**

- [ ] a. car was being repossessed by the lender that financed its purchase
- [ ] b. car was being towed because it was parked illegally
- [ ] c. police had a warrant to seize the car as evidence in a criminal investigation
- [x] **d. tow truck operator was unlawfully removing the car**

> **Explanation:** Theft is a covered cause of loss, but confiscation by a government authority is specifically excluded from coverage.
