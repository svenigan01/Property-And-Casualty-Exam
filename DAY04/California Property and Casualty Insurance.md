# CA Property and Casualty Agent Exam - California Property and Casualty Insurance - Legal Concepts of Tort Law

### Question 1
**Long before the fatal accident occurred, Jose knew his truck's brakes were failing, but he kept driving while sending text messages on his phone. Given such egregious behavior, the dead motorist's survivors could properly accuse Jose of:**

- [ ] **a.** Assault with a deadly weapon.
- [x] **b. Gross negligence.**
- [ ] **c.** Ordinary negligence.
- [ ] **d.** Slight negligence.

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **b**  
> Jose's complete disregard for others' safety appears to be willful and wanton enough to qualify as gross negligence.

### Question 2
**Dwayne works for a drug store. He sees Joel, a customer, carrying a large bag and acting suspicious, so Dwayne suspects Joel of shoplifting. Without creating a disturbance, Dwayne quietly asks Joel to go to the store's office where Dwayne keeps him until the police arrived. When they search Joel and his bag, they do not find any stolen merchandise. Joel sues Dwayne and the store, accusing them of:**

- [x] **a. False arrest.**
- [ ] **b.** Gross negligence.
- [ ] **c.** Libel.
- [ ] **d.** Slander.

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **a**  
> From Joel's perspective, detaining Joel was an unlawful restraint on his freedom. If Dwayne had accused Joel in front of other shoppers, he might also have been sued for slander.

### Question 3
**Which one of the following is not an intentional tort?**

- [ ] **a.** False arrest
- [ ] **b.** Libel
- [ ] **c.** Slander
- [x] **d. Sleeping on the job**

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **d**  
> An intentional tort is a deliberate act that causes harm to another person, such as assault, battery, libel, slander, false arrest, and invasion of privacy. Barring extenuating circumstances, sleeping on the job does not meet these criteria.

### Question 4
**The Podunk Press published an anonymous letter to the editor in which the writer stated that the world would end on a certain date. That date passed and the world did not end. Although the published statement was false, it is not libel because:**

- [ ] **a.** In publishing this statement, the newspaper intended no harm.
- [ ] **b.** Libel consists of spoken false statements, and this statement was delivered in writing.
- [ ] **c.** Libel is an unintentional tort, and this publication was intentional.
- [x] **d. The statement did not damage a person's reputation.**

> **Explanation / Correct Answer:** **d**  
> Libel, an intentional tort, is a written or printed untrue statement that intentionally or unintentionally damages a person's reputation.
