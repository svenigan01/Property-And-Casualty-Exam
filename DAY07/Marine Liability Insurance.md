# Property & Casualty Insurance - Marine Liability Insurance - Protection and Indemnity (P&I) Marine Liability Insurance

---

### Question 1
**Protection and indemnity policies generally provide shipowners with broad liability coverage against which one of the following?**

- [ ] **a.** damage to passengers' baggage
- [x] **b.** injury to crew members  
  > **Explanation:** Protection and indemnity policies cover injury to crew members and longshoremen. Most marine claims arise from these exposures.
- [ ] **c.** loss of the ship's cargo
- [ ] **d.** loss resulting from towing a vessel that is not in distress

---

### Question 2
**Casey determines that an event aboard his ship is probably covered under his protection and indemnity policy. In compliance with the conditions regarding claims under the P&I policy, what should Casey do?**

- [x] **a.** cooperate with the underwriters in defending the claim  
  > **Explanation:** Casey is expected to cooperate with his underwriters in defending the claim.
- [ ] **b.** immediately admit liability
- [ ] **c.** take over the insurer's rights of recovery
- [ ] **d.** wait at least one year before submitting the claim

---

### Question 3
**A shipowner may be held liable for injury to various people or damage to property in various categories. Which of these presents the costliest exposure and generates the most claims?**

- [ ] **a.** damage to cargo
- [ ] **b.** damage to other vessels
- [x] **c.** injury to crew members and longshoremen  
  > **Explanation:** A shipowner's major and most costly exposure is to crew members and longshoremen, and most claims are generated from these exposures. Loss or damage to cargo is not covered under a P&I policy.
- [ ] **d.** injury to passengers

### Question 4
**Jamaal was injured when he tripped over a rope on the deck of the Reyna while sailing on the high seas. If Jamaal is to collect damages under the Jones Act, he must be able to demonstrate all the following facts EXCEPT:**

- [ ] **a.** Jamaal was a seaman at the time of the accident.
- [ ] **b.** Jamaal was employed by the Reyna's owner or operator.
- [ ] **c.** Jamaal's injuries can be attributed to unsafe conditions on the ship.
- [x] **d.** Jamaal's injuries were caused by another seaman's negligence.  
  > **Explanation:** The Jones Act does not require proof that another seaman's negligence caused the injuries. Moreover, because the fellow servant rule is eliminated, the shipowner cannot use a fellow employee's negligence as a defense.
