### Question 1
**Suzette has a $1 million personal umbrella policy with a $250 retained limit (deductible) for personal liability and a personal auto policy (PAP) with a $300,000 limit. She is at fault in an auto accident that is settled for $500,000. How will this settlement amount be paid?**

- [ ] **a.** Suzette will pay $250, the PAP insurer will pay $300,000, and the umbrella insurer will pay $199,750.
- [ ] **b.** Suzette will pay nothing, and the PAP insurer and the umbrella insurer will both pay $250,000.
- [x] **c.** Suzette will pay nothing, the PAP insurer will pay $300,000, and the umbrella insurer will pay $200,000.  
  > **Explanation:** The primary PAP insurer will pay its policy limit of $300,000, and the umbrella insurer will pay the remaining $200,000 balance. The retained limit (deductible) only applies to losses covered by the umbrella policy that are *not* covered by underlying primary insurance.
- [ ] **d.** The umbrella insurer will pay the entire $500,000 settlement since it is within the $1 million umbrella limit.

# Insurance Residual Market – Practice Questions

## 1. Which statement is correct about insurance plans obtained through residual market mechanisms in comparison to those obtained on the voluntary market?

* **a. Consumers pay higher rates and receive more limited benefits.** ✅ **Correct Answer**

  * Residual market mechanisms make insurance available to consumers who cannot obtain it in the voluntary market. Consumers usually pay **higher rates** in a residual market and receive **somewhat limited benefits**.

* b. Consumers pay lower rates and receive more limited benefits.

* c. Consumers pay lower rates and receive the same benefits.

* d. Consumers pay the same rates and receive more benefits.

### Key Point

**Residual Market = Higher Rates + Limited Benefits**

---

## 2. At least four different types of state-based auto residual market plans are available, but the majority use which of the following mechanisms?

* **a. Assigned risk plan (automobile insurance plan)** ✅ **Correct Answer**

  * Automobile insurance plans, also known as **assigned risk plans**, are currently found in **43 states and the District of Columbia**.

* b. Joint underwriting association (JUA)

* c. Reinsurance facility

* d. State fund

### Key Point

**Majority of auto residual markets = Assigned Risk Plans**

---

## 3. Which insurance submarket presents insurance companies with the greatest risks?

* a. Nonstandard market

* b. Preferred market

* **c. Residual market** ✅ **Correct Answer**

  * The residual market consists of consumers who, for various reasons, cannot obtain insurance in the voluntary market. Insurers who insure them typically issue policies with the **highest rates and most limited benefits**.

* d. Standard market

### Key Point

**Greatest Risk to Insurers = Residual Market**

---

## 4. What do FAIR (Fair Access to Insurance Requirements) plans provide?

* a. A residual market for workers compensation insurance
* b. Auto insurance for designated drivers subject to financial responsibility requirements
* c. Auto insurance on hard-to-insure drivers in certain states
* **d. Property insurance on hard-to-insure property in certain areas** ✅ **Correct Answer**

  * FAIR plans provide **property insurance** to property owners in qualifying areas who cannot otherwise obtain property insurance in the regular market.

### Key Point

**FAIR Plan = Hard-to-Insure Property**
