# Property & Casualty Insurance - Commercial General Liability Insuracne - Commercial General Liability (CGL) Policy

### Question 1
**The swimming pool in the atrium of the Highland Court Apartment Building creates a humid environment that encourages mold growth. To limit exposure to mold-related claims, what kind of endorsement should be added to the building's CGL policy?**

- [ ] a. fungi or bacteria exclusion
- [x] **b. limited fungi or bacteria coverage**
- [ ] c. limited pollution liability extension
- [ ] d. pollution liability exclusion

> **Explanation:** The limited fungi or bacteria coverage endorsement subjects mold coverage to a separate fungi and bacteria liability aggregate limit.

---

### Question 2
**What does the limited pollution liability extension endorsement do when added to the CGL insurance policy?**

- [ ] a. It broadens the policy's definition of pollutants.
- [ ] b. It excludes pollution coverage.
- [x] **c. It expands pollution coverage.**
- [ ] d. It limits pollution coverage to losses arising from bacteria and fungi.

> **Explanation:** The endorsement replaces the CGL pollution liability exclusion with broader coverage for pollution.

### Question 3
**Lisa's CGL policy from Equitable Insurance Company expires. Equitable's premium auditor asks to review Lisa's records in accordance with the policy's premium audit condition. Why does Equitable want to audit her records?**

- [x] **a. to determine the earned premium based on the actual exposures**
- [ ] b. to determine the sources of funding for the premium payments
- [ ] c. to determine whether the premium is priced competitively
- [ ] d. to ensure that the premium is not discriminatory

> **Explanation:** A premium audit allows the insurer to determine the earned premium based on the insured's actual exposures during the policy period and whether the premium paid is appropriate for the risk. This may result in an additional charge or a partial refund.

### Question 4
**Jonathan is the sole proprietor of a shoe repair business. His wife, Laura, is the bookkeeper. Jonathan is the only named insured listed in the business' commercial general liability insurance policy. Laura is also a business partner with her sister Amy in a restaurant. Who is an insured under the shoe shop's CGL policy?**

- [ ] a. Jonathan alone
- [ ] b. Jonathan and Laura, with respect to all business activities
- [x] **c. Jonathan and Laura, with respect to the shoe business**
- [ ] d. Jonathan, Laura, and Amy, with respect to all business activities

> **Explanation:** When a sole proprietor is a named insured, his or her spouse is also insured with respect to the named insured's business.
