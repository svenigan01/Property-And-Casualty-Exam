# Property & Casualty Insurance - Workers Compensation Insurance - Workers Compensation 

### Question 1
**Newton, a garbage truck driver, breaks a hip when he falls from the cab. Doctors expect a full recovery, but Newton must remain in bed with the hip immobilized until the cast is removed. Newton's condition would be categorized as a:**

- [ ] a. permanent partial disability
- [ ] b. permanent total disability
- [ ] c. temporary partial disability
- [x] **d. temporary total disability**

> **Explanation:** Newton is expected to recover from the injury and return to employment, but he is unable to do any type of work while he is recovering because he is temporarily totally disabled.

---

### Question 2
**Justin is employed by Quarry Works, Inc. After ten years of working in various positions, he learns that he has silicosis. To ensure his condition will qualify as an occupational disease under most workers compensation laws, what must Justin demonstrate?**

- [x] **a. The condition arose out of and in the course of his employment.**
- [ ] b. The condition specifically relates to his most recent job tasks.
- [ ] c. The condition was caused by the negligence of another employee.
- [ ] d. The condition was caused by the negligence of his employer.

> **Explanation:** The general rule is that, for occupational diseases to be compensable, there must be a direct causal link between the employment and the disease.

---

### Question 3
**In Andrea's state, all employers are required to buy workers compensation coverage through a state workers compensation insurance fund. The state where Andrea's business is located has what kind of fund or plan?**

- [ ] a. competitive state fund
- [x] **b. monopolistic state fund**
- [ ] c. state residual market plan
- [ ] d. voluntary workers compensation fund

> **Explanation:** As the state's only workers compensation insurance market, the state fund in Andrea's state has a monopoly on workers compensation insurance, and the funds are referred to as monopolistic.

---

### Question 4
**Bradley Corporation, a large business with excellent loss experience, wants to self-insure its workers compensation exposures rather than purchase commercial insurance. In most states, Bradley will NOT be permitted to self-insure unless it:**

- [ ] a. authorizes the state to act as Bradley's agent for service of suits
- [ ] b. provides evidence that self-insurance has the approval of the employees' union
- [x] **c. provides satisfactory proof that Bradley can meet its obligations to injured workers**
- [ ] d. requires Bradley's non-union employees to sign a waiver restricting their benefits

> **Explanation:** Almost all states permit an employer to self-insure workers compensation provided that, among other requirements, the employer proves it is able to directly pay required benefits to injured workers.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Workers Compensation Insurance - Workers Compensation and Employers Liability Insurance

### Question 1
**What determines the classification code that the insurer uses in computing a business's workers compensation premium rate?**

- [ ] a. how long the business has been in operation
- [ ] b. the geographic location of the business
- [x] **c. the nature of the business's operations**
- [ ] d. the size of the business

> **Explanation:** The type of business operation an employer conducts determines the appropriate classification of covered employees.

---

### Question 2
**Which of the following employers would most likely have the highest workers compensation rate classification?**

- [x] **a. factories**
- [ ] b. radio stations
- [ ] c. retail merchants
- [ ] d. schools

> **Explanation:** Classifications categorize employers who have common types of exposures. Any type of factory is going to have a higher (more expensive) rating than a business employing clerical and white-collar workers.

---

### Question 3
**Workers compensation insurance rates apply per what unit of payroll?**

- [ ] a. $1,000
- [ ] b. $10
- [ ] c. $10,000
- [x] **d. $100**

> **Explanation:** Workers compensation insurance rates apply per $100 of payroll.

---

### Question 4
**ABC Company's insurance agent has informed ABC that its current workers compensation policy has a 1.3 experience modifier but its renewal policy will have a 1.1 "mod" which means:**

- [ ] a. ABC's loss experience was better than average and is getting even better.
- [ ] b. ABC's loss experience was better than average but is getting worse.
- [ ] c. ABC's loss experience was worse than average and is getting worse.
- [x] **d. ABC's loss experience was worse than average but is getting better.**

> **Explanation:** An experience modification factor (mod) greater than 1.0 indicates that the employer's loss experience during the relevant period was worse than that of others in the same class. In this case, the lower renewal mod indicates that ABC's experience is improving and the premium will decrease.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Crime Insurance and Bonds - Commercial Crime Policy

### Question 1
**Private School's headmaster was distressed when he learned that Agnes, a full-time teacher, helped students cheat on a college entry exam. He scolded Agnes privately but, to avoid a scandal, did nothing further. A few months later he caught Agnes stealing school equipment and made a claim under the school's employee dishonesty coverage. Is the loss covered?**

- [ ] a. No, because employee theft coverage applies only to theft of money.
- [x] **b. No, because the coverage for Agnes ended when the headmaster learned of her first dishonest act.**
- [ ] c. Yes, because a full-time teacher is an employee.
- [ ] d. Yes, because employee theft coverage applies to property other than money.

> **Explanation:** Coverage under any employee theft insuring agreement immediately terminates with respect to a given employee once a manager learns of any dishonest act by that employee.

---

### Question 2
**When an insurance policy provides crime coverage on a loss sustained basis, coverage is triggered by a loss that occurs:**

- [ ] a. 90 days after the policy period ends
- [ ] b. after the retroactive date and is discovered during the policy period
- [ ] c. during the policy period and is discovered at any time after the policy period has ended
- [x] **d. during the policy period and is discovered within one year after the policy period ends**

> **Explanation:** Loss sustained coverage applies to loss that occurs during the policy period and is discovered within one year after the policy period ends. Discovery-based coverage applies to loss that is discovered during the policy period or within 60 days after the policy period ends.

---

### Question 3
**The crime insurance policy Jodie is applying for will provide blanket coverage against employee theft. What does this mean?**

- [x] **a. Coverage will apply to theft by any of Jodie's employees.**
- [ ] b. Jodie has to list the specific employees that she wants coverage for.
- [ ] c. Jodie has to list the specific positions or job titles for which coverage will apply.
- [ ] d. The limit shown in the declarations also applies to all the policy's coverages.

> **Explanation:** Blanket coverage means that coverage applies to theft by any individual meeting the "employee" definition. Positions, titles, or names of the employees need not be listed.

---

### Question 4
**Car Dealer was offering a free TV with the purchase of any new car. When a man in the showroom picked up a TV and carried it out of the showroom, the sales manager held the door open, assuming the man had just bought a car. He had not. What is Car Dealer the victim of?**

- [ ] a. burglary
- [ ] b. embezzlement
- [ ] c. robbery
- [x] **d. theft**

> **Explanation:** No break-in or break-out was involved as is required by the traditional definition of burglary, but the man was in the showroom to steal property. No force or threat of force was involved in this act of stealing, so it was not a robbery. Embezzlement requires that victim place the person stealing the property in a position of trust to handle the property.

# Property & Casualty Insurance - Crime Insurance and Bonds - Fidelity and Surety Bonds

### Question 1
**When the court appointed Delia as guardian for her late sister's children's financial assets, it also required her to furnish a fiduciary bond. The fiduciary bond does NOT guarantee that Delia will:**

- [ ] a. be accountable for any deficits
- [ ] b. execute these duties in good faith
- [ ] c. faithfully account for all property
- [x] **d. raise the children in their parents' religious faith**

> **Explanation:** A fiduciary bond guarantees that the fiduciary will exercise duties faithfully, account for all property, and make good for any deficiency.

---

### Question 2
**In connection with an upcoming construction project in which it hopes to participate, a contractor furnishes a performance bond guaranteeing that:**

- [x] **a. the contractor will perform the work in accordance with the construction contract**
- [ ] b. the project owner will receive a mechanics lien if suppliers are not paid by the contractor
- [ ] c. the project owner will receive an amount equal to the surety's liability under the bond if the contractor fails to pay its workers
- [ ] d. the work will be performed at the price quoted in the bid that was submitted to obtain the contract

> **Explanation:** A bid bond guarantees that work will be performed at the price quoted in a bid to obtain the contract. A performance bond guarantees that the contractor will perform the work in accordance with the construction contract. A payment bond guarantees that the contractor will pay its workers and suppliers, thus avoiding a mechanics lien being placed on the project owner's property.

---

### Question 3
**Through a fidelity bond issued by Omega Assurance, Olga is guaranteed that her employee, Dee, will not be dishonest. In this transaction, Olga is the:**

- [x] **a. obligee**
- [ ] b. obligor.
- [ ] c. principal
- [ ] d. surety

> **Explanation:** The obligee is the party, usually the employer, to whom the surety makes the guarantee. Olga is the obligee, Omega is the surety, and Dee is the principal.

---

### Question 4
**Linwood requires each of its public officials to furnish a public officials bond. These bonds will normally terminate:**

- [ ] a. a year from their date of issue
- [ ] b. if Linwood does not pay the renewal premium
- [ ] c. when its aggregate limits are exhausted by payment of two or more settlements or judgments
- [x] **d. when the bonded public official leaves office**

> **Explanation:** Public officials bonds usually terminate only when successors have been elected or appointed or the current official leaves office for any reason.
